'passed' here does NOT mean pumpAndSettle settled. The probe wraps the call
in try/catch and prints its verdict, so the test passes either way - passing
only means the probe itself ran.

The probe's print() output goes to logcat, not to patrol's stdout, and was
not captured. So the PROBE-40 verdict lines are NOT available and this run
is suggestive, not conclusive.

What the durations suggest: the settle timeout is 25s. If one policy had
settled and the other timed out, the two tests would differ by roughly 25s.
They differ by 5s and are both ~1m50s, which is what you get when BOTH time
out at 25s on top of ~80s of fixed boot and native-automation overhead.

That matches the source analysis in criterion-1-cause-in-source.txt, and it
is why the probe's original decision rule was wrong. That rule said:

    onlyPumps settles + fadePointers times out -> the binding is the cause.
    both time out                              -> the app is the cause.

The second line is false. onlyPumps re-arms the next frame exactly as
fadePointers does - the re-arming branch at flutter_test binding.dart:2823
excludes only 'benchmark'. So 'both time out' is the EXPECTED result of a
binding cause, and reading it as an app cause would send the next reader
hunting a bug in lib/ that is not there.

The conclusive evidence for this issue is the source, not this run.
