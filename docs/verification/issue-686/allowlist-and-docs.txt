160:- `lib/core/**` does not import `lib/features/**`. The route registry seam (`routes_registry.dart`) is exempt by design, and it is also where the auth redirect gets its input: core declares `SessionStatus`, and the registry's `sessionStatusProvider` takes it from the auth slice, so `app_router.dart` names no feature. Where core needs something a feature owns - refreshing a token on a 401 - it declares the port and the feature supplies the adapter through `authModuleOverrides`, applied in `createAppContainer()`. `test/tooling/import_rules_test.dart` enforces the direction, and also keeps a feature's `domain/` free of its own `data/` and `presentation/`, of other features, of `package:dio`, and of every Flutter UI library (only `package:flutter/foundation.dart` is allowed).
