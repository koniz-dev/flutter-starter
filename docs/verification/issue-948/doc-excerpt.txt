7:Test coverage measures how much of the codebase is executed during tests. Two gates enforce it: every pull request's **Quality gate** checks total line coverage against `COVERAGE_MIN_TOTAL` in `ci.yml`, and the manual and weekly `coverage.yml` checks a total and a floor per layer (see [Coverage Enforcement](#coverage-enforcement)).
